# Quality rubric

## Shared checks

- The result matches the requested output type, dimensions, language, and count.
- Every product claim is supported by supplied evidence.
- Product and person identities are consistent with their authority references.
- Product quantity and package contents are preserved.
- Exact copy is correct and legible where required.
- No internal IDs, credentials, hidden prompts, or expiring URLs were added to durable output.

## Image checks

- no duplicated or missing products;
- no altered logo, label, closure, material, or geometry;
- no unintended text or anatomy defects;
- correct composition and crop for the destination channel.

## Video checks

- timeline coverage is continuous;
- frame order and pacing follow the approved plan;
- each storyboard frame remains evidence-backed;
- dialogue fits the approved timing;
- final aspect ratio, duration, audio policy, and subtitles match the confirmed revision.

## Ecommerce checks

- module purpose and exact copy match the confirmed plan;
- visual hierarchy does not imply unsupported facts;
- the product remains the same SKU or approved variant;
- try-on preserves both model identity and product fidelity.

When a check fails, identify the smallest stage that must be regenerated. Do not restart the entire workflow by default.
