# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want the AIDesigner Open Workflow community to be welcoming, factual, and useful. Participants should:

- be respectful and professional;
- discuss ideas and code rather than attacking people;
- welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- provide reproducible evidence when reporting technical problems;
- protect private data, credentials, and customer information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets, and knowingly misleading or malicious reports.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

To report a conduct concern, contact the project owner through <https://aidesigners.art> with the relevant context. Do not include secrets or private customer data.
